bored is a completely free bot and relies completely on donations to stay up and running. While donations are fully optional, they are always welcome and appreciated.

<Callout type="info" title="What donations currently provide">
Donating does not unlock real bot benefits at the moment. The only current perk is a donator role in the bored Discord server. Join the server and send proof of donation there if you want the role. That may change in the future if donor benefits are added later.
</Callout>

You can join the support server here: [bored.rest/discord](https://discord.com/invite/UePr2KBcFY)
