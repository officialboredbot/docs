AntiNuke helps you keep moderators and other trusted members within strict safety boundaries. If someone starts abusing destructive administrative actions and exceeds your configured threshold, bored immediately applies your chosen punishment before widespread damage occurs.

## How AntiNuke works

Each AntiNuke module tracks a specific action type and counts occurrences within a sliding time window. Once that counter reaches the threshold you set, bored executes the configured punishment, creates a security log case, and alerts the server owner.

## Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run setup">
    This applies bored's recommended security preset.

```text
/antinuke setup
```

    It enables protection for dangerous permission grants, channel creation and deletion, role deletion, expression deletion, mass bans, mass kicks, webhook creation, and unauthorized bot joins. Vanity protection can be enabled separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the status">
    View your active protection matrix, whitelist, and protected roles/members.

```text
/antinuke config
```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Bulk toggle">
Use `/antinuke toggle status:on` or `/antinuke toggle status:off` to quickly toggle all modules without losing your customized thresholds or punishments.
</Callout>

## Administrators & Whitelist

The **server owner** is an AntiNuke admin by default. Server owners can designate additional trusted admins who are exempt from AntiNuke enforcement and can configure protection settings.

<Callout type="info" title="bored vanity">
The companion [**bored vanity**](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1459869503289561293) bot is permanently whitelisted so vanity role rewards never trigger AntiNuke.
</Callout>

### Add or remove an admin

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antinuke admin target:<user>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antinuke admin target:@trusted-mod
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### View admins

```text
/antinuke admins
```

### Whitelist a member or bot

Whitelisted members and bots bypass AntiNuke restrictions completely.

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antinuke whitelist target:<user>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antinuke whitelist target:@trusted-bot
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Whitelist all existing bots

Bulk-whitelist bots already in the server so legitimate utility bots are not penalized:

```text
/antinuke whitelistbots
```

## AntiNuke Protect

Protect critical roles or individual users from kicks and bans. If an unauthorized moderator attempts to kick or ban a protected target, bored punishes the actor immediately. This is ideal for your **Server Booster** role or VIP members.

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example", "List"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antinuke protect target:<role or member> [punishment:<punishment>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antinuke protect target:@Server Booster punishment:stripstaff
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antinuke protect view_list:true
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
