bored's media engine applies high-performance transformations across images, animated GIFs, and video clips. Media sources can be supplied via direct attachment upload, image URL, or member avatar.

## Captions and meme templates

<Tabs items={["Caption", "Speechbubble", "Ah shit"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/media caption text:<string> [attachment:<attachment>] [url:<string>] [member:<user>] [bottom:<string>] [black:<string>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/media speechbubble [attachment:<attachment>] [url:<string>] [member:<user>] [solid:<string>] [flip:<string>] [bottom:<string>] [scale:<number>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/media ahshit [attachment:<attachment>] [url:<string>] [member:<user>]
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
