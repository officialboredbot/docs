AntiRaid focuses on join-based threats and automated bot raids. It detects join spikes, disposable throwaway accounts, and default-avatar spammers before they settle into your community.

## Mass join protection

Monitors rapid join floods. When too many accounts join in a short timeframe, AntiRaid trips, enters temporary raid state, locks channels, and bans upcoming join attempts.

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid massjoin setting:<on or off> [threshold:<number>] [action:<kick or ban>] [lock_channels:<true or false>] [punish:<true or false>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid massjoin setting:on threshold:5 action:ban lock_channels:true punish:true
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When massjoin triggers, bored turns **raid state** on automatically. While raid state is active, system welcome and goodbye messages are paused. Raid state clears automatically once the trigger batch has been resolved.

## Default avatar filtering

Automatically penalize accounts that join with no custom profile picture (default Discord avatar).

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid avatar setting:<on or off> [action:<kick or ban>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid avatar setting:on action:kick
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## New account age restrictions

Block accounts that were created too recently to prevent throwaway alt raids.

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid newaccounts setting:<on or off> [threshold:<minimum days>] [action:<kick or ban>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid newaccounts setting:on threshold:7 action:ban
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

In this example, accounts younger than `7` days are immediately banned upon joining.

## External apps lockdown

Strips the **Use External Apps** permission across all managed server channels for `@everyone` to prevent unauthorized user-app spam and webhooks during raids.

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid apps [excluded:<roles>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid apps excluded:@Staff @Trusted
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Whitelist

AntiRaid whitelist entries are single-use tokens consumed when that specific user joins, allowing trusted friends or alternate accounts to bypass filters once.

### Whitelist a member

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid whitelist toggle member:<user>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid whitelist toggle member:@trusted-member
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### View whitelist entries

```text
/antiraid whitelist view
```

## Management & Controls

### View settings

```text
/antiraid config
```

### Manual raid state

Enable or disable raid state manually. Turning it off clears the punish window and unlocks channels locked by AntiRaid.

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid state setting:<on or off>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid state setting:off
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Global toggle

Quickly toggle all AntiRaid modules on or off without altering saved parameters:

```text
/antiraid toggle setting:on
```

### Reset configuration

Disables all AntiRaid modules, clears the punish window, turns raid state off, and unlocks locked channels.

```text
/antiraid reset
```

## Post-raid cleanup

If a raid occurred before filters were configured, purge accounts that joined within a specific recent time window:

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid raid time:<duration> action:<kick or ban> reason:<reason>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/antiraid raid time:2h action:ban reason:raid cleanup
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
