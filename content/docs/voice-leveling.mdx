Voice leveling tracks time members spend in voice channels and turns it into XP, voice levels, reward roles, and leaderboard placement.

Members earn voice XP while they stay in a non-ignored voice channel. bored awards **10 XP per minute** before your server's XP multiplier is applied, and checks about once per minute while someone remains in voice.

<Callout type="warning" title="Disabled by default">
**Voice leveling** stays off until someone with **Manage Server** enables it with `voicelevel enable`.
</Callout>

## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable voice leveling">
    Turn the system on first.

```text
voicelevel enable
```

    Members can start gaining voice XP after this is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add reward roles or a level-up channel">
    Optional setup for roles at specific voice levels or level-up posts in a text channel.

```text
voicelevel role 5 @Voice Legend
```

```text
voicelevel channel #voice-levels
```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable stream tracking separately">
    Stream and camera time use the `stream` command group and stay off until you enable them.

```text
stream enable
```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check a voice rank card

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel [member]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel @member
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Use this without a member to view your own **Voice Level**, server rank, XP progress, total voice time, and session count.

## Turn voice leveling on or off

### Enable voice leveling

```text
voicelevel enable
```

This turns voice leveling on for the server and allows members to start gaining voice XP.

### Disable voice leveling

```text
voicelevel disable
```

This turns voice leveling off until it is enabled again.

## Reward roles

Reward roles let bored assign a saved role when someone reaches a voice level you choose.

### View reward roles

```text
voicelevel roles
```

Use this to review every configured reward role together with its required level and total XP.

### Add a reward role

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel role (level) (role)
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel role 5 @Voice Legend
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

In the example above, members receive `@Voice Legend` when they reach voice level `5`. The role must sit below bored's highest role.

### Remove a reward role

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel roleremove (role)
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel roleremove @Voice Legend
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

This removes that role from the voice level reward list.

### Stack reward roles

```text
voicelevel stackroles
```

When stacking is on, members keep earlier voice reward roles as they level up. When stacking is off, bored keeps only the highest reward role it can manage.

### Sync reward roles

```text
voicelevel sync
```

Run this after changing reward roles or stacking behavior if you want bored to re-apply the current setup across the server.

## Level-up channel

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel channel [channel]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel channel #voice-levels
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Run `voicelevel channel` with no channel to clear the level-up notification channel. When a member levels up in voice, bored posts in the channel you set here.

## Ignored voice channels

### Toggle an ignored channel

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel ignore (channel)
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel ignore #afk-voice
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Run the same command again to stop ignoring that channel. Time in ignored channels does not count toward voice XP or sessions.

## Crownboard

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel crownboard (channel)
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel crownboard #general
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

bored posts a live embed in that channel listing the top voice members. The crownboard refreshes every **10 minutes**.

## Leaderboard

### Open the leaderboard

```text
voicelevel leaderboard
```

This shows the top members in the server by total voice time, with level and XP on each line. Results are paginated when the list is long.

## Config panel

```text
voicelevel config
```

This opens bored's voice level config panel so you can review whether voice leveling is on, stack roles, the XP multiplier, the level-up channel, crownboard channel, reward roles, and ignored channels in one place.

## Reset and cleanup

### Reset one member

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel reset (member)
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
voicelevel reset @member
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

This clears that member's voice XP and updates their reward roles.

### Reset all voice level data

```text
voicelevel resetconfig
```

This requires **Manage Server** and confirmation. bored clears all voice level data, reward roles, ignored channels, and crownboard settings for the server, then disables voice leveling.

## Stream and camera tracking

Stream tracking is separate from voice XP. It records how long members stream or use camera while they are in voice.

<Callout type="warning" title="Disabled by default">
**Stream tracking** stays off until someone with **Manage Server** enables it with `stream enable`.
</Callout>

### View stream stats

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
stream [user]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
stream @member
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Use this to view current activity, stream time, camera time, and session counts. If the member is live in voice, bored includes time from their current session.

### Enable stream tracking

```text
stream enable
```

This turns stream and camera tracking on for the server.

### Disable stream tracking

```text
stream disable
```

This turns stream and camera tracking off until it is enabled again.

### Open the stream leaderboard

```text
stream leaderboard
```

This ranks members by combined stream and camera time.
