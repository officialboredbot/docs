Voice leveling tracks time members spend connected to voice channels, awarding voice XP, milestone reward roles, and updating live crownboard displays.

Members earn **10 XP per minute** while connected to non-ignored voice channels.

## Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable voice leveling">
    Voice leveling is disabled by default. Enable it with:

```text
/voicelevel enable
```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your voice stats">
    View your total voice hours, rank, and XP progress:

```text
/voicelevel stats
```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reward roles

Automatically award roles when members reach specific voice levels.

### Add a reward role

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel role [level:<integer>] [role:<role>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel role level:5 role:@Voice Legend
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### View & remove reward roles

<Tabs items={["List roles", "Remove role"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel roles
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel roleremove role:@Voice Legend
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Stack roles & sync

<Tabs items={["Stack roles", "Sync roles"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel stackroles
```

Toggles role stacking so members either retain previous milestone roles or keep only their highest.

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel sync
```

Backfills voice roles for all active members.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Channel notifications

Configure where voice level-up notifications are dispatched:

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel channel [channel:<channel>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel channel channel:#voice-levels
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Omit the channel parameter to clear the announcement channel.

## Ignored channels

Prevent XP gain in AFK or muted voice channels:

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel ignore channel:<channel>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel ignore channel:#afk-voice
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Leaderboards & Crownboards

### Live crownboard

Post an automatically updating voice leaderboard embed:

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel crownboard channel:<channel>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel crownboard channel:#general
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Refreshes every **10 minutes**.

### Server leaderboard

```text
/voicelevel leaderboard
```

## Settings & Reset

View configuration:

```text
/voicelevel config
```

Reset a single member or wipe all voice level data:

<Tabs items={["Reset member", "Reset server"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel reset user:@member
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel resetconfig
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Screen share & camera tracking

Track when members stream games or turn on video in voice rooms.

<Tabs items={["Enable", "Disable", "Stats", "Leaderboard"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel stream enable
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel stream disable
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel stream stats [user:<user>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/voicelevel stream leaderboard
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
