## Inactivity

Inactive servers that haven't invoked any of bored's commands for a long time.

<Callout type="info">
You can reinvite bored by clicking [here](https://bored.rest/invite).
</Callout>

## Blacklisted

If a server gets caught violating Discord's or bored's Terms of Service, it can be blacklisted, and bored will leave and your server will be blacklisted, meaning bored cannot be readded.

<Callout type="info">
If you think that was a mistake, contact [support](https://bored.rest/discord).
</Callout>
