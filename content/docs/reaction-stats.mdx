---
title: Reaction Stats
description: Track received reaction counts for up to three emojis and inspect member leaderboards.
section: Miscellaneous
icon: face-smile
---

Reaction Stats counts reactions members receive on their messages once enabled. When another member adds a tracked reaction, the author's tally increments. Self-reactions are ignored, and removing a reaction deducts from the balance. Up to **3** emojis can be tracked simultaneously.

## Setup and tracked emojis

<Tabs items={["Enable", "Disable", "Add emoji", "Remove emoji", "List"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats enable
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats disable
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats add emoji:<emoji>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats remove emoji:<emoji>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats list
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Check member counts and leaderboard

<Tabs items={["Member stats", "Leaderboard"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats user [member:<user>] [emoji:<string>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats leaderboard [emoji:<string>]
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Bot filtering and channel rules

<Tabs items={["Bots", "Ignore", "Whitelist"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats bots count:<on|off>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats ignore [action:<add|remove|list|reset>] [channel:<channel>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server reactstats whitelist [action:<add|remove|list|reset>] [channel:<channel>]
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When bot counting is disabled, reactions from bot accounts and reactions placed on bot messages are discarded.
