Suggestions can post directly to a public channel or pass through a private staff review queue first.

## Setup

```text
suggest setup (channel) [review channel]
```

The first channel receives approved suggestions. When a review channel is provided, new submissions wait there for staff approval.

```text
suggest config
suggest channel (channel)
suggest review (channel or off)
suggest enable
suggest disable
```

These settings require **Manage Server**.

## Submit

```text
suggest (idea)
suggest submit (idea)
```

Members can also reply to a message and run `suggest` without text to submit that message.

## Voting and threads

```text
suggest emoji (upvote emoji) (downvote emoji)
suggest threads (on or off)
```

Discussion threads are created on suggestion posts when enabled.

## Staff statuses

```text
suggest approve (suggestion ID or message)
suggest deny (suggestion ID or message)
suggest consider (suggestion ID or message)
suggest progress (suggestion ID or message)
suggest pending (suggestion ID or message)
suggest status (status) (suggestion ID or message)
```

Staff can use a suggestion ID, message link, or a reply to the suggestion message.

## Ignore members and roles

```text
suggest ignore (member or role)
suggest ignore add (member or role)
suggest ignore remove (member or role)
suggest ignore list
suggest ignore reset
```

Ignored members cannot submit suggestions. Ignored roles apply to every member who holds that role.
