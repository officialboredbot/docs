Word Stats analyzes and counts normalized vocabulary frequencies across server messages. Command invocations are ignored, and bot accounts can be included or excluded on demand.

## Enable tracking

<Tabs items={["Enable", "Disable", "Include bots"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/server wordstats enable
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server wordstats disable
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server wordstats bots enabled:<on|off>
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Word leaderboard

View the top counters for a specific term across the server.

<Tabs items={["Syntax", "Example"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/server wordstats leaderboard word:<string> [page:<integer>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server wordstats leaderboard word:bored page:1
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Channel filtering

Restrict or exclude specific text channels and categories.

<Tabs items={["Ignore", "Whitelist"]}>
  <Tab>

```text
/server wordstats ignore [action:<add|remove|list|reset>] [channel:<channel>]
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab>

```text
/server wordstats whitelist [action:<add|remove|list|reset>] [channel:<channel>]
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When any channel is whitelisted, word tracking operates exclusively within whitelisted destinations.
